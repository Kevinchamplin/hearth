A few honest details

"On your device, ~1 GB, best in Chrome." Here's what that means.

🏠 It lives on your computer

The thinking happens right on your own machine — not on a faraway company's server. That's why it's private, and why it keeps working with the internet switched off. It's your AI, like any other app on your computer.

📦 Why the one-time download

You're bringing the whole "brain" home once — instead of borrowing it over the internet every time you ask something. Think of downloading a movie for a flight instead of streaming it: the first load is big, then it's instant forever, even offline.

🧭 Why Chrome or Edge

To think quickly, Hearth uses your computer's graphics chip — the same part that makes games and video look smooth. Today, Chrome and Edge let it do that; Safari and Firefox are catching up. So for the smoothest ride right now, open it in Chrome or Edge.

The only catch — and we'll name it ourselves: your first visit downloads Hearth's brain. A few minutes, one time. That is the entire price — no money, no account, no fine print. Like downloading a movie before a flight: once it's home, it's yours. Put plainly: a few minutes from now, you own an AI.

Keep it forever: install it like a real app

Hearth can live in your Dock or taskbar — no app store needed. Pick your browser and we'll point right at the button.

  1. Open Hearth in Chrome and light the fire.
  2. Look at the right end of the address bar for a small install icon — a little screen with a down-arrow.
  3. Click it, then Install. Done — Hearth now opens from your Dock (Mac) or Start menu (Windows), in its own window.

Don't see the icon? Click the ⋮ menu in Chrome's top corner → Cast, save and share → Install page as app… — or just use the ⬇ Install button inside Hearth itself.

  1. Open Hearth in Microsoft Edge.
  2. Click the ⋯ menu in the top-right corner.
  3. Choose Apps → Install Hearth. It now opens from your Start menu like any other app.

Edge may also show a small install icon at the right end of the address bar — one click does the same thing.

  1. Open Hearth in Safari.
  2. Click File in the menu bar at the top of your screen.
  3. Choose Add to Dock… and click Add.

Honest heads-up: Hearth runs smoothest in Chrome or Edge today — Safari is still catching up on the technology Hearth uses to think fast.

  1. iPhone & iPad: open Hearth in Safari, tap the Share button (the square with an arrow), then Add to Home Screen.
  2. Android: open Hearth in Chrome, tap the ⋮ menu, then Add to Home screen (or Install app).

Honest heads-up: phones are still experimental for Hearth — newer phones with plenty of memory handle the Fast brain; older ones may struggle. A computer is the best seat by the fire for now.

The browser version is live now — the Mac, Windows & Linux apps are in active, in-the-open development. Star it on GitHub to follow along.
