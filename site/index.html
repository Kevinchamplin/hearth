A few honest details

"On your device, ~1 GB, best in Chrome." Here's what that means.

🏠 It lives on your computer

The thinking happens right on your own machine — not on a faraway company's server. That's why it's private, and why it keeps working with the internet switched off. It's your AI, like any other app on your computer.

📦 Why the one-time download

You're bringing the whole "brain" home once — instead of borrowing it over the internet every time you ask something. Think of downloading a movie for a flight instead of streaming it: the first load is big, then it's instant forever, even offline.

🧭 Why Chrome or Edge

To think quickly, Hearth uses your computer's graphics chip — the same part that makes games and video look smooth. Today, Chrome and Edge let it do that; Safari and Firefox are catching up. So for the smoothest ride right now, open it in Chrome or Edge.

One honest note: Hearth isn't trying to beat ChatGPT or Gemini — those run on enormous data centers. Hearth runs on your computer, and that's exactly the point: simple, fast, and completely private. It won't build you a full website, but it'll happily give you a recipe, explain something tricky, help you word an email, or brainstorm — right on your machine, free, with nobody watching.
